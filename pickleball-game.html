Pickleball

You against the computer. You hit from the left, it hits from the right. Seen from above, played sideways.

You and a computer partner against two.
Opponents6 (3.5)
Your partner6 (3.5)
Level 1 is the easiest, 5 plays like a 3.0, 6 like a 3.5, 8 like a 4.5, 10 like a pro. Lower levels also give you a wider window to hit a drop.
Tap Play to go full screen. Turn your phone sideways to play.
Controls. Left thumb: the stick sits in the corner, drag its knob to move. Right thumb: HIT is a drive, SOFT is a dink or drop, LOB goes over their head. Lean the stick up or down as you hit to aim. For a dink, lean toward the net as you hit for a quicker one, or back for a soft one. When you serve the buttons become SHORT, DRIVE and SLICE.
Rules. Serve underhand to the diagonal box. Serve and return must bounce. No volleys inside the kitchen (the green strip). Side out scoring: only the serving side scores. To 11, win by 2. Doubles is called server score, receiver score, server number.
