Pickleball

Singles against the computer. You hit from the left, it hits from the right. Seen from above, played sideways.

Tap Play to go full screen. Turn your phone sideways to play.
Controls. Left thumb: drag anywhere on the left half to move. Right thumb: HIT is a drive, SOFT is a dink or drop, LOB goes over their head. Lean the stick up or down as you hit to aim.
Rules. Serve underhand to the diagonal box. Serve and return must bounce. No volleys inside the kitchen (the green strip). Rally scoring to 11, win by 2.
